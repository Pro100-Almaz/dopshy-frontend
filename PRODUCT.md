# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences share one system. Today the primary users are **arena operators** — managers, coaches, accountants, and floor staff who run Dopsy Arena day-to-day through the admin panel: taking bookings, managing field slots and schedules, tracking payments, and coordinating the academy and staff. As the product develops, focus shifts toward **players booking fields** directly through the public flow (browse fields → pick a 5×5 or 6×6 slot → checkout), most often on their phones. Roles in the system: super_admin, manager, coach, accountant, staff.

## Product Purpose

Dopsy Arena is the operating tool for a football turf venue. It replaces the scattered mix of spreadsheets, calls, and messaging apps with one place to fill fields and run the arena. Success is concrete: **maximum field occupancy with minimum admin effort** — every hour of turf sold, every booking confirmed and paid without a staff member chasing it manually.

## Positioning

The fastest way to fill a football pitch — book a slot in seconds, and run the whole arena without spreadsheets.

## Brand Personality

Fast and sporty. The product should feel energetic and confident, echoing the arena itself — turf, floodlights, momentum. Interactions are quick and punchy; nothing lingers. Voice is direct and Russian-language, spoken like a coach who respects your time: brief, decisive, no filler. Confident, never loud; sporty, never gimmicky.

## Anti-references

Must not read as a generic, off-the-shelf admin template — which is exactly what it is under the hood (TailAdmin Vue). The anonymous SaaS-dashboard look (default blue-and-gray chrome, cream neutrals, interchangeable card grids) is the thing to design away from. Give it the arena's own identity so no one mistakes it for a stock template.

## Design Principles

Book in a breath — the shortest path from intent to a confirmed, paid slot always wins. Cut taps, defaults over blank fields, no dead ends.

Operators glance, players tap — staff screens carry dense operational truth read at a glance; player screens stay effortless and thumb-first. Same system, two levels of density.

Earn the turf — carry the arena's sporty confidence into product surfaces. Distinctive over safe, but always in service of the task, never decoration for its own sake.

Occupancy is the North Star — surface what fills fields (open slots, demand, fast rebooking) and flag what empties them (unpaid, cancellations, gaps). The interface should make the money-metric visible.

Mobile is the real venue — the player-facing flow is designed phone-first, with real touch targets and small-screen layouts, because that's where booking actually happens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: verified contrast on text and controls, full keyboard navigation, and a reduced-motion alternative for every animation. Mobile-first — generous touch targets and layouts that hold up on small screens, since players book on phones.
